Swing City

SWING CITY

a web-slinging delivery sim

press Enter or tap anywhere

Click the game first so it gets your keys. Hold SPACE for auto-web · click a building for a manual web · A/D or arrows to steer · W/S to reel in/out · hold SHIFT to hang upside down · R to reset everything (cash, costumes, deliveries). M to mute. Watch the windows: shooters fire at you, so dodge the bullets! You have 3 lives, and running out costs you all your cash. On touch: hold a finger down to web. Goal: follow the beam, grab the 📦 on a rooftop, then drop it at the green pad for cash. Faster delivery = more money. Press U to spend your cash on costumes and web designs.
