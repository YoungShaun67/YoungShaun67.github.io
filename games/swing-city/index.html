ABOUT THE GAME

Swing between skyscrapers with real pendulum physics through a pixel-art city. Hold on, let go at the perfect moment, and carry your momentum from rooftop to rooftop.

HOW TO PLAY

HOLD LMB

SWING

Hold the left mouse button to swing.

RELEASE LMB

LET GO

Release the left mouse button to release.

W / S

REEL

Press W to reel in and S to reel out.

A / D

STEER

Use A and D to control your swing.

Also: hold SPACE for auto-web, hold SHIFT to hang upside down, press R to restart. On touch screens, hold a finger down to web.
